ELECTRICAL REPAIRS

Find the problem before deciding on the repair.

Electrical problems can originate from different parts of an installation. A proper repair process therefore begins with understanding the reported symptoms and assessing the affected electrical system.

TNSE Electrical Projects can provide fault finding, troubleshooting, diagnosis and corrective repair support according to the condition of the system and the agreed project scope.

FAULT INVESTIGATION

Electrical problems can have different causes.

A reported electrical problem does not always indicate that a particular component is responsible. Investigation helps establish what may be causing the issue before repair work is undertaken.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Understand the symptom. Trace the problem. Plan the repair.

Troubleshooting focuses on understanding the reported electrical symptom and systematically investigating the affected system.

1. Understand the Symptom

Establish what customer reported, when it occurs, and system part affected.

2. Investigate

Inspect electrical system and investigate possible causes according to scope.

3. Correct

Complete agreed repair or recommend further work where problem requires action.

COMPONENT REPAIRS

Repair, replace or investigate further.

Once the fault has been assessed, the appropriate action may involve repairing a component, replacing an affected component or carrying out further diagnosis.

The recommended approach depends on the condition of the component, the wider electrical system and the requirements of the project.
